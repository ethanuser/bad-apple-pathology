# Source credits

**Histology.** The Genotype-Tissue Expression (GTEx) project, supported by the Common Fund of the Office of the Director of the National Institutes of Health and by NCI, NHGRI, NHLBI, NIDA, NIMH and NINDS. The H&E whole-slide images were collected by the GTEx Biospanel and Biospecimen Research teams and are distributed in DICOM by the NCI Imaging Data Commons under CC BY 4.0. Fedorov et al., *National Cancer Institute Imaging Data Commons*, RadioGraphics 43 (2023). [IDC GTEx collection](https://portal.imaging.datacommons.cancer.gov/collections/gtex); [DICOM conversion record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11099099); [GTEx Portal](https://gtexportal.org)

**Anatomy.** Human Reference Atlas 3D reference organs (Visible Human male and female, Allen brain), HuBMAP Consortium, CC BY 4.0. Börner et al., *Human BioMolecular Atlas Program (HuBMAP): 3D Human Reference Atlas construction and usage*, Nature Methods (2025). Organ placements come from the HRA CCF API scene used by the Exploration User Interface. [3D reference organ library](https://hubmapconsortium.github.io/ccf/pages/ccf-3d-reference-library.html); [HRA](https://humanatlas.io)

**Sampling sites.** GTEx tissue-block registrations, entered by Kristin Ardlie for the GTEx project with the HRA Registration User Interface, from the hra-registrations repository (MIT licence). [hra-registrations/gtex](https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/hra-registrations/tree/main/gtex)

**Icons.** Material Symbols by Google, Apache License 2.0. [material-design-icons](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons)

**Adapted imagery.** The histology fields, slide thumbnails and anatomy renders in this project are available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Changes to the slides: stain separation, cropping, uniform scaling, in-plane rotation, optional mirroring, a soft tone curve set by one threshold per field and, for white-on-black frames, re-colouring of hematoxylin and eosin as emission on black. Changes to the anatomy: rendering, colouring and selection of organs. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

**Interface.** The three layouts follow the conventions of real slide viewers (QuPath, Aperio WebViewer DX and Hamamatsu NDP.view2) as shown in their public documentation. No logos or product names appear in the video, and the renderer was written for this project.

**Animation and sound.** *Bad Apple!!* shadow PV by Anira. Music by Alstroemeria Records, featuring nomico, based on the composition by ZUN / Team Shanghai Alice. The animation and soundtrack retain their creators' rights. Public uploads require an appropriate basis for using those works alongside the CC-licensed material. [Shadow PV](https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm8628149); [Pinned encode](https://github.com/bad-apple-lab/Bad-Apple/tree/80e39ed58d754e4de420b7a1c474e53e20a71ba3)

**Companion project.** The search, scoring and temporal sequencing are adapted from [Bad Apple!! but every frame is a medical scan](https://github.com/ethanuser/bad-apple-medical-scan).
